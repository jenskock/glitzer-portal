# Illustrations-Stil „Das Glitzer-Portal“

Alle Bilder entstehen mit einem Bildmodell. Jeder Prompt ist so aufgebaut:
**STIL + FIGUREN (nur die, die im Bild vorkommen) + SZENE + REGELN.**
Als Referenzbilder werden immer `characters/lineup.png` und `illustration/stil-referenz-ds1.jpg` mitgegeben (freigegebener Stil, Doppelseite 1).

## STIL (immer gleich, wörtlich übernehmen)
Children's picture book double-spread illustration, wide landscape 16:9, high-quality cute storybook art: soft painterly digital illustration with clean outlines, warm saturated colors, gentle natural lighting, lots of charming small details, like a modern premium picture book for 6-8 year olds. Match the art style of the attached style reference image exactly. Use the attached character lineup as the strict reference for character design (keep shapes, colors and accessories identical, rendered in the same soft, cute storybook quality as the style reference). Characters are large, expressive and clearly readable.

## FIGUREN (Textbausteine)
- **Lotti:** Lotti the pink axolotl: pink skin, frilly darker-pink gills (three per side), big friendly smile, yellow tinkering goggles on her forehead, turquoise backpack with brown straps (a wrench and a yellow star poking out).
- **Bruno:** Bruno the capybara: warm caramel-brown, lighter belly and snout, big blunt nose, tiny ears, a small hair curl on top, red neckerchief with white dots; brave, kind protector.
- **Paula:** Paula, a girl of about 7-8 with brown skin: two dark braids with yellow hair ties, pink headband with a yellow flower, turquoise T-shirt, orange shorts, yellow rubber boots.
- **Tuko:** Tuko the toucan: black feathers, cream-white chest, blue eye rings, huge colorful beak (green, orange, red tip).
- **Momo:** Momo the sloth: beige-brown fur, light face with dark eye stripes, sleepy half-closed eyes, long arms with claws, pink flower at her ear.
- **Fips:** Fips the hummingbird: shiny green feathers, red throat, long thin beak, light-blue blurred buzzing wings, small.
- **Rudi:** Rudi the raccoon: grey fur, black eye mask, white eyebrows, ringed tail, one buck tooth, carries a golden spoon and a brown sack full of found objects; cheeky but not evil.
- **Portal:** the water portal: a round sparkling turquoise water whirlpool inside a ring of stones with glowing turquoise dots, glitter around it.

## REGELN (immer anhängen)
Mood: cheerful and safe, even in exciting scenes. Leave calmer areas (sky or water) where text can sit if needed. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no speech bubbles in the image. Keep every character's design consistent with the reference.

## Sonderfälle
- **Suchbild:** Szene wie die Story-Seite, die Objekte klein aber erkennbar an den im Manuskript genannten Stellen. Danach Hotspot-Koordinaten (Prozent) für die App notieren.
- **Fehlerbild:** zuerst Bild A erzeugen, dann Bild B mit Bild A als Referenz und genau den 5 Änderungen. Die Unterschiede prüfen und notfalls per Bildbearbeitung exakt setzen.
- **Ausmalbild:** "clean black line-art coloring page for children, thick even outlines, closed shapes, no shading, white background", gleiche Figuren.
- Format: 16:9 erzeugen, für die App als WebP (~1600 px breit) exportieren.
